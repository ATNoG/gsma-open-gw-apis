\chapter{Conclusion}

Throughout this project, we ran into some problems and unexpected issues. But
these problems turned into a valuable learning experience. They helped us get a
much better grasp of how the 5G network works on the inside. Furthermore, one
major thing we learned was how important it is to have a gateway that's both
standardized and simple, which in part led us to learning about working on a
world of standards, where we had to follow a specific set of requirements. When
a gateway is set up this way, it makes it much easier for users to interact
with the 5G network. This also helps make integration and development much
smoother and more straightforward.

Besides gaining technical knowledge, this project also helped us sharpen our
problem-solving skills. We often had to deal with situations that weren’t
planned. Changing our approach when needed became part of how we worked. Being
flexible like this was key. It allowed us to stay on track and still meet our
goals even when we faced obstacles.

As a result, we managed to put in place the subset of the Open Gateway’s APIs
we planned for and connected them smoothly with IT’s 5G infrastructure. To pull
this off, we worked closely with the main parts of the gateway. We also had to
take on extra tasks that weren’t part of our original plan. For example, we
used and modified the NEF Emulator, which by the end was working as an actual
NEF communicating with the 5G core, and deployed a SMSC fully integrated in
IT's infrastructure. Both of these were important to make sure everything
worked properly and fit together as expected.

By going beyond what was first required, we didn’t just improve what the
project could do. We also grew our own skills and understanding of how 5G
systems operate in real-world settings. This thorough approach has better
prepared us to handle future projects in similar complicated environments and
led to a strong and successful outcome, where we were able to present a
comprehensive demonstration of the utility and importance of these types of
gateways.
