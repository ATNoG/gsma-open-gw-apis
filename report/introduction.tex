\chapter{Introduction}

\section{Context}

The development of 5G networks and beyond 5G have introduced advanced
capabilities in the access, reconfiguration and flexibility of these networks.
This attracts the interest of software developers in several industries, aiming
to offer integrated solutions to their clients that leverage these capabilities
of the network and their operators. However, the existing interfaces defined by
the \emph{3rd Generation Partnership Program} (\emph{3GPP}) expose much of the
complexity related to the networks and the operators. This complexity hinders
the development of integrated solutions and requires extensive knowledge on the
topic of networks and how to operate them, limiting the adoption.

The \emph{GSMA Open Gateway} is an industry initiative that aims to solve this
problem. GSMA proposes a collection of Application Programming Interfaces
(APIs) defined by the \emph{CAMARA} project, which exposes various simplified
interfaces of the operator networks. These APIs can then be offered either
through the MNOs directly or by aggregators, such as \emph{Hyperscalers}.

However, most operators do not offer said interfaces, but it is to be expected
that they will do so in the near future. Given the capabilities of
\emph{Instituto de Telecomunicações} (IT) in Aveiro, with access to a 5G
infrastructure \cite{5GAIner}, there is interest in integrating \emph{GSMA Open
	Gateway} APIs in their offer.

\section{Goals}

The objectives outlined for the project were as follows:

\begin{enumerate}
	\item Implementation of the following \emph{GSMA
		      Open Gateway} \emph{APIs} through integration with
	      \emph{IT's} 5G Core.
	      \begin{itemize}
		      \item \emph{API} family to obtain \emph{Device Information}
		      \item \emph{API} family to obtain information on the network's
		            \emph{Quality-of-Service} (\emph{QoS}) and to offer
		            \emph{Quality-on-Demand} (\emph{QoD}).
	      \end{itemize}

	\item Implementation of the \emph{GSMA Open Gateway} \emph{API} to obtain and
	      verify \emph{One Time Passwords} (\emph{OTPs}) through \emph{Short Message
		      Services} (\emph{SMS}), utilizing an \emph{SMS Center} (\emph{SMSC})
	      integrated in \emph{IT's} 5G Network as a part of our project.

	\item Offering location services through the \emph{Network Exposure Function} interface
	      (\emph{NEF}, explained in more detail in section
	      \ref{sec:related_work_3gpp}) . Since
	      \emph{IT's} network currently lacks a native \emph{NEF} implementation, we
	      employed the \emph{NEF Emulator} project and enhanced it progressively
	      until it closely resembled the behavior and functionality of a real
	      \emph{NEF}.

	\item Integration of our project with \emph{OpenSlice}
	      (\emph{OSL})\cite{openslice}, an \emph{Operation Support System}
	      (\emph{OSS}), which provides management of 5G Network services and the
	      orchestration of cloud resources. This integration enables support for
	      a \emph{CAMARA-as-a-Service} (\emph{CAMARAaaS}) business model by
	      allowing efficient exposure, provisioning, and lifecycle management of
	      network APIs, thereby simplifying integration with existing services.
\end{enumerate}
